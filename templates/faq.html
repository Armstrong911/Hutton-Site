Frequently Asked Questions

Submit a maintenance request using our online form. Include as much detail as possible and we will follow up within one business day.
Strata fees are set annually by the strata council based on the approved budget. They cover operating expenses and contributions to the contingency reserve fund.
Documents such as minutes, bylaws, and financial statements can be requested through our strata documents form. Standard turnaround is 5–10 business days.
Form K is a Notice of Tenant's Responsibilities under the Strata Property Act. It must be completed when an owner rents their unit. Submit it through our Form K page.
Complaints regarding noise, bylaw violations, or neighbour disputes can be submitted through our complaint form. All submissions are reviewed by the property manager.
Complete our Pre-Authorized Debit form and we will set up automatic monthly payments from your bank account. Fees are withdrawn on the first of each month.
New owners should complete the Owner Registration form as soon as possible after taking possession. This ensures you receive important strata communications.
