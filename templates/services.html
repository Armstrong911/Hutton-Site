Our Services

Hutton Property Management provides comprehensive strata management services across Victoria, BC.

Strata Management

Full-service management of strata corporations including meeting coordination, bylaw enforcement, owner communication, and day-to-day operations.

Financial Administration

Complete financial management including budget preparation, monthly reporting, contingency reserve fund planning, and year-end financial statements.

Maintenance Coordination

Coordination of routine maintenance, emergency repairs, and capital projects. We manage contractor relationships and ensure quality work at competitive rates.

Owner & Tenant Services

Support for owners, tenants, realtors, and lenders including document requests, Form K processing, estoppel certificates, and move-in coordination.

Insurance Management

Coordination of strata insurance including annual renewals, claims management, and ensuring adequate coverage for the corporation.
